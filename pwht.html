Service Overview

Practical inspection support for critical industrial assets

IAIS UAE provides post-weld heat treatment programmes using controlled heating, calibrated thermocouples and recorded thermal cycles. PWHT is applied where welding procedures, material requirements or project specifications require controlled post-weld thermal treatment.

Our team scopes the inspection around the asset, access conditions, project requirements and required reporting. The goal is clear, traceable field information that supports maintenance, quality and integrity decisions.
