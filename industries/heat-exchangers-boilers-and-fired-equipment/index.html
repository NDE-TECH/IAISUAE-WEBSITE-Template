Application & Asset Overview

Heat exchangers, boilers and fired equipment

Assessment of tubes, pressure parts, welds and heat-affected components exposed to thermal, corrosive and process-related deterioration. Within hydrocarbon production, processing, transportation and export operations, this asset can directly influence pressure containment, corrosion control, process safety and production continuity. A focused integrity programme should therefore connect the design basis, materials, service history, previous findings and current operating condition, so that inspection effort is directed to credible damage locations and produces information that supports practical maintenance decisions.

Integrity Focus

Principal considerations and IAIS UAE support

Principal integrity considerations

  • Tube-wall loss, pitting, erosion, fretting and inlet-end attack.
  • Tube-to-tubesheet or pressure-boundary leakage, cracking and joint deterioration.
  • Fouling, deposits, thermal cycling and support damage that can reduce heat-transfer performance.

How IAIS UAE may support

  • Select tube and component techniques according to material, geometry, cleanliness, access and expected defect morphology.
  • Screen the bundle and pressure boundary, then target suspect areas for confirmation and dimensional assessment.
  • Rank affected tubes or components and provide repair, plugging, replacement or monitoring inputs.
Outputs & Requirements

Typical outputs and applicable standards

Typical outputs

Tube condition maps, signal and indication summaries, component NDT records, defect distribution, comparison data and recommendations.

Codes & standards

The activity may be planned and reported against the applicable provisions of API 510, API 570, API 653, API 579-1/ASME FFS-1, API 580/581, ASME BPVC, ASME B31.3/B31.4/B31.8, ISO, ASTM and client requirements. Final references and acceptance criteria remain subject to approved project and statutory requirements.
