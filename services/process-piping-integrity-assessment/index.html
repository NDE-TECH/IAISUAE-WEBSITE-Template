Process Piping Integrity Assessment

Process Piping Integrity Assessment is delivered to prevent loss of containment by focusing inspection on corrosion circuits, stress raisers, high-risk fittings and support-related deterioration. IAIS UAE develops the scope around the equipment design, materials, service environment, operating history, expected damage mechanisms and the client’s required decision. This prevents technique-led inspection from becoming disconnected from asset risk and ensures that findings are traceable, technically relevant and suitable for maintenance or engineering review.

Parent Service Category 06

Piping & Pipeline Integrity

This category provides integrity support for process piping, utility systems, plant pipelines and transmission lines throughout construction, operation, maintenance and repair. Piping systems contain many branches, fittings, dead legs, injection points, supports, insulated sections and welded joints where service conditions can concentrate degradation. IAIS UAE organises the scope by corrosion circuit, damage mechanism and line criticality so that inspection locations are technically justified and repeatable.

✓

Extensive line populations with limited inspection resources and incomplete circuit or thickness-history data.

✓

Localised corrosion, erosion, CUI, vibration fatigue, cracking and mechanical damage concentrated at specific features.

✓

Screening long or inaccessible sections while recognising the limitations of indirect techniques.

✓

Translating wall-thickness and weld data into remaining strength, repair priorities and future inspection intervals.

Assets and applications

Process and utility piping, cross-country pipelines, station piping, transfer lines, manifolds, branches, dead legs, injection points, insulated circuits, supports, flanges and welded joints.

Category outcome

Clear, traceable technical information that supports safety, compliance, reliability, maintenance prioritisation and lifecycle value.

IAIS UAE Delivery Approach

How this category is delivered

The following delivery and application information is taken from the approved parent-category content master.

01

IAIS UAE delivery approach

The programme may include API 570 planning, circuitisation, pipeline condition review, thickness measurement, corrosion mapping, guided-wave screening, CUI assessment, focused dead-leg and injection-point inspection, small-bore and branch examination, support surveys, weld inspection and flange/bolting assessment. Results are compared with design requirements and historical data to establish corrosion rates, minimum remaining thickness and action priorities.

02

Assets and applications

Process and utility piping, cross-country pipelines, station piping, transfer lines, manifolds, branches, dead legs, injection points, insulated circuits, supports, flanges and welded joints.

03

Service position

Process Piping Integrity Assessment is one of 16 approved services within Piping & Pipeline Integrity.

Client Value & Coverage

Category-level value and deliverables

01

Value to the client

Clients obtain a location-controlled basis for managing loss-of-containment risk, selecting repairs, prioritising insulation removal and setting appropriate monitoring or inspection intervals. The approach also improves the quality of isometrics, circuit registers and historical data for future integrity programmes.

02

Typical category deliverables

Piping/pipeline register; corrosion circuits; inspection plan; marked-up isometrics; thickness and corrosion maps; guided-wave or weld reports; CUI and support findings; corrosion rates; remaining-strength inputs; repair recommendations and interval assessment..

03

Category outcome

Clear, traceable technical information that supports safety, compliance, reliability, maintenance prioritisation and lifecycle value.
