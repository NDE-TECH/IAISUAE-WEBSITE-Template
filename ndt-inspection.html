Service Overview

Practical inspection support for critical industrial assets

IAIS UAE provides non-destructive testing for surface and sub-surface discontinuity detection without damaging the inspected component. The service supports fabrication quality control, shutdown inspection, in-service integrity assessment and maintenance programmes across the UAE and GCC.

Our team scopes the inspection around the asset, access conditions, project requirements and required reporting. The goal is clear, traceable field information that supports maintenance, quality and integrity decisions.
