FMC/TFM Ultrasonic Imaging: Capabilities and Industrial Use

Full Matrix Capture records transmit-receive combinations across an array, while Total Focusing Method reconstructs focused images within a defined region.

Technical principle and inspection purpose

Full Matrix Capture records transmit-receive combinations across an array, while Total Focusing Method reconstructs focused images within a defined region. A competent team should define the intended result before mobilisation so equipment capability, scanning coverage and reporting detail are aligned with the client’s decision.

For FMC TFM inspection, the technical principle and inspection purpose stage should document the inspection objective, target flaw, material and component geometry. This stage must remain linked to the asset and original data so later reviewers can distinguish verified condition, interpreted indications, assumptions and areas where further evidence is required.

Where the technique creates value

Applications include complex welds, small or difficult geometries, flaw characterisation and high-resolution imaging where validated configurations offer an advantage. Data quality depends on controlled calibration, repeatable location referencing, suitable surface or access conditions and preservation of the original inspection evidence.

For FMC TFM inspection, the where the technique creates value stage should document the asset locations, access method, expected coverage and operating constraints. This stage must remain linked to the asset and original data so later reviewers can distinguish verified condition, interpreted indications, assumptions and areas where further evidence is required.

Planning and procedure requirements

Wave set, grid, pixel size, probe, frequency, wedge, acoustic model, region of interest and calibration reflectors must be selected for the component and target. Where confidence is limited, the report should recommend verification, complementary NDT, expanded coverage or engineering assessment rather than presenting an unsupported conclusion.

For FMC TFM inspection, the planning and procedure requirements stage should document the approved procedure, calibration basis, essential variables and personnel responsibilities. This stage must remain linked to the asset and original data so later reviewers can distinguish verified condition, interpreted indications, assumptions and areas where further evidence is required.

Important limitations and complementary methods

Large datasets and visually detailed images can create false confidence. Model error, mode conversion, grating effects, material anisotropy and poor validation remain important constraints. Applicable codes and standards provide a framework, but the governing document and edition must be established for the specific asset and contract.

For FMC TFM inspection, the important limitations and complementary methods stage should document the unexamined zones, detection boundaries, uncertainty and required complementary checks. This stage must remain linked to the asset and original data so later reviewers can distinguish verified condition, interpreted indications, assumptions and areas where further evidence is required.

From inspection evidence to an engineering decision

FMC/TFM should be qualified against representative flaws or reference reflectors and reported with the reconstruction parameters required for technical review. For industrial facilities in the UAE and GCC, selection must also reflect operating temperature, environmental exposure, access restrictions, shutdown windows and the owner’s technical requirements.

For FMC TFM inspection, the from inspection evidence to an engineering decision stage should document the evaluation basis, indication disposition, follow-up action and retained evidence. This stage must remain linked to the asset and original data so later reviewers can distinguish verified condition, interpreted indications, assumptions and areas where further evidence is required.
