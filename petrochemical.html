Sector Overview

Inspection planned around the risks and assets of Petrochemical

Petrochemical plants combine complex process equipment, aggressive service conditions and tight turnaround windows. IAIS UAE supports inspection planning and field execution with NDT, corrosion monitoring, PWHT and integrity-management services.

Scopes can combine field inspection, difficult-access methods, materials testing and integrity support depending on the equipment condition, maintenance window and reporting requirement.
