Sector Overview

Inspection planned around the risks and assets of Manufacturing

IAIS UAE supports manufacturers and fabricators with NDT, material verification, mechanical testing and metallurgical services. Inspection can be integrated into fabrication stages so quality issues are identified before final release.

Scopes can combine field inspection, difficult-access methods, materials testing and integrity support depending on the equipment condition, maintenance window and reporting requirement.
