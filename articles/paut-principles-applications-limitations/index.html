Phased Array Ultrasonic Testing: Principles, Applications and Limitations

It is widely applied to welds, corrosion mapping, nozzles, forgings and components where multiple angles or repeatable imaging improve coverage and interpretation. Important limitations and complementary methods

Beam steering cannot overcome every restriction. Near-surface zones, taper, counterbore, anisotropic weld metal, restricted scanning surfaces and unfavourable flaw orientation require specific evaluation. A competent team should define the intended result before mobilisation so equipment capability, scanning coverage and reporting detail are aligned with the client’s decision.

From inspection evidence to an engineering decision

Properly acquired PAUT data supports location, characterisation and sizing, but evaluation must remain tied to the approved procedure and applicable acceptance criteria.
