Vendor Inspection

Vendor Inspection is delivered to detect, locate and characterise relevant discontinuities or degradation using a technique appropriate to the examination objective. IAIS UAE develops the scope around the equipment design, materials, service environment, operating history, expected damage mechanisms and the client’s required decision. This prevents technique-led inspection from becoming disconnected from asset risk and ensures that findings are traceable, technically relevant and suitable for maintenance or engineering review.

Service 159 in the IAIS UAE Elaborated Service-Page Content Master, Final Version 1.0 dated 15 August 2026.
Parent Service Category 11

Quality, Vendor & Project Inspection

This category provides independent surveillance and verification throughout procurement, fabrication, installation, testing, commissioning and handover. The objective is to confirm that materials, workmanship, processes, tests and documentation comply with approved purchase, code and project requirements before equipment or work progresses beyond critical stages. IAIS UAE can support individual source visits or provide continuous quality surveillance across vendors, sites and project packages.

✓

Complex purchase specifications and inspection plans with unclear hold, witness, review and surveillance responsibilities.

✓

Material, welding, testing or documentation nonconformities discovered too late for efficient correction.

✓

Schedule risk caused by delayed submittals, incomplete records, unresolved punch items or poor manufacturing visibility.

✓

Maintaining independence, traceability and consistent reporting across multiple vendors, locations and disciplines.

Assets and applications

Pressure equipment, piping, valves, fabricated structures, skids, tanks, machinery packages, castings, forgings, project materials, site installations and multidisciplinary handover systems.

Category outcome

Clear, traceable technical information that supports safety, compliance, reliability, maintenance prioritisation and lifecycle value.

IAIS UAE Delivery Approach

How this category is delivered

The following delivery and application information is taken from the approved parent-category content master.

01

IAIS UAE delivery approach

IAIS UAE may review ITPs and procedures, verify incoming materials and traceability, witness fabrication, welding, NDT, dimensional checks, heat treatment and pressure tests, perform vendor/source inspection, monitor progress, complete pre-shipment inspection and support installation, commissioning and handover. Observations and nonconformities are documented against specific requirements and tracked to an agreed disposition or closure status.

02

Assets and applications

Pressure equipment, piping, valves, fabricated structures, skids, tanks, machinery packages, castings, forgings, project materials, site installations and multidisciplinary handover systems.

03

Service position

Vendor Inspection is one of 16 approved services within Quality, Vendor & Project Inspection.

Client Value & Coverage

Category-level value and deliverables

01

Value to the client

Clients gain earlier visibility of conformity and progress, reducing the probability of rejected equipment, site rework, documentation gaps and delayed commissioning. Independent records also strengthen release decisions, supplier performance review and final project acceptance.

02

Typical category deliverables

ITP and document review; visit and surveillance reports; material/welding/test verification; nonconformity and punch list; progress/expediting status; release note; pre-shipment report; commissioning records; handover dossier review and completion verification..

03

Category outcome

Clear, traceable technical information that supports safety, compliance, reliability, maintenance prioritisation and lifecycle value.
