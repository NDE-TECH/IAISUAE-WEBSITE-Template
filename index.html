About IAIS UAE

A Standards-Driven Partner for Industrial Asset Integrity

IAIS UAE extends the inspection expertise of IAIS India Integrity & Advanced Inspection Solutions UAE is part of the IAIS Group, established to support industries across the UAE and GCC with internationally recognised engineering codes, inspection standards and asset-integrity practices.

We help asset owners protect critical equipment, maintain regulatory and technical compliance, prevent unexpected failures and extend operational life through structured, reliable and technically accountable solutions.

01

International Codes & Standards

Our technical solutions are developed, evaluated and delivered in accordance with applicable international codes, standards, recommended practices and classification requirements issued by API, ASME, ISO, ASTM, AWS, IEC, NFPA, AMPP/NACE, EEMUA, EN, BS, IMO and IACS member classification societies.

02

Complete Asset-Lifecycle Support.

We support industrial assets from design review, fabrication and commissioning through operation, maintenance, integrity assessment, repair validation and life-extension planning.

03

Regional Presence, Group Expertise.

Our UAE operations are supported by the IAIS Group’s multidisciplinary engineering expertise, technical resources and field capabilities, enabling dependable project execution throughout the UAE and wider GCC.
