Service Overview

Practical inspection support for critical industrial assets

IAIS UAE mechanical-testing support helps verify that materials and weld procedure samples meet specified mechanical-property requirements. Testing may include tensile, Charpy impact, hardness and bend tests with results documented for engineering and QA/QC review.

Our team scopes the inspection around the asset, access conditions, project requirements and required reporting. The goal is clear, traceable field information that supports maintenance, quality and integrity decisions.
