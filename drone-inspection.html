Service Overview

Practical inspection support for critical industrial assets

IAIS UAE drone inspection supports visual and thermal data capture on flare stacks, tank roofs, pipe racks and offshore structures. UAV inspection can reduce the need for hazardous access and helps engineering teams document visible condition, thermal anomalies and asset geometry.

Our team scopes the inspection around the asset, access conditions, project requirements and required reporting. The goal is clear, traceable field information that supports maintenance, quality and integrity decisions.
