Sector Overview

Inspection planned around the risks and assets of Renewable Energy

IAIS UAE applies inspection methods used across industrial assets to renewable-energy infrastructure. Programmes can include structural NDT, drone visual surveys, foundation checks and balance-of-plant inspection for new and operating assets.

Scopes can combine field inspection, difficult-access methods, materials testing and integrity support depending on the equipment condition, maintenance window and reporting requirement.
