Refractory Dry-Out

Refractory Dry-Out is delivered to achieve and demonstrate the required thermal cycle while controlling gradients, heating rates, soak temperature and cooling. IAIS UAE develops the scope around the equipment design, materials, service environment, operating history, expected damage mechanisms and the client’s required decision. Parent Service Category 09

PWHT, Heat Treatment & Thermal Services

This category provides controlled heating, soaking and cooling of welds, components and refractory systems to meet specified metallurgical, fabrication or process requirements. Successful heat treatment depends on more than reaching a target temperature: heating rates, temperature gradients, soak duration, thermocouple placement, insulation, power distribution and cooling must be controlled and documented. IAIS UAE develops the arrangement from the material, thickness, geometry, welding procedure and applicable thermal cycle.

✓

Uneven geometry and heat losses producing unacceptable gradients or incomplete heated-band control.

✓

Incorrect thermocouple number, placement or attachment affecting the reliability of recorded temperatures.

✓

Power, cable, insulation and equipment-capacity limitations at field locations.

✓

Demonstrating that the complete cycle complied with the approved procedure and resolving interruptions or deviations.

Assets and applications

Piping welds, pressure vessels, pipelines, heavy-wall components, structural welds, castings, fabricated assemblies, furnaces, boilers and refractory-lined equipment.

Category outcome

Clear, traceable technical information that supports safety, compliance, reliability, maintenance prioritisation and lifecycle value.

IAIS UAE Delivery Approach

How this category is delivered

The following delivery and application information is taken from the approved parent-category content master.

01

IAIS UAE delivery approach

The service may include local or furnace PWHT, resistance or induction heating, preheat/post-heat, stress relieving, controlled heating/cooling, refractory dry-out, thermal-cycle monitoring, thermocouple installation, chart recording, hardness verification, procedure preparation, equipment rental and technical manpower. Heating zones, control zones and monitoring points are planned before mobilisation, and the cycle is supervised with calibrated equipment and traceable records.

02

Assets and applications

Piping welds, pressure vessels, pipelines, heavy-wall components, structural welds, castings, fabricated assemblies, furnaces, boilers and refractory-lined equipment.

03

Service position

Refractory Dry-Out is one of 16 approved services within PWHT, Heat Treatment & Thermal Services.

Client Value & Coverage

Category-level value and deliverables

01

Value to the client

Clients receive a controlled and auditable thermal process that supports weld quality, dimensional stability, hydrogen control, residual-stress reduction and code compliance. Clear planning reduces the risk of cycle rejection, rework, schedule disruption and disputes over temperature records.

02

Typical category deliverables

Heat-treatment procedure; equipment and power plan; heating/insulation layout; thermocouple map; calibration records; time-temperature charts; cycle compliance report; deviation record; hardness results and equipment/manpower mobilisation documentation..

03

Category outcome

Clear, traceable technical information that supports safety, compliance, reliability, maintenance prioritisation and lifecycle value.
