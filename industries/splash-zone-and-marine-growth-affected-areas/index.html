Application & Asset Overview

Splash-zone and marine-growth affected areas

Inspection planning for components exposed to cyclic wetting, accelerated corrosion, coating damage and restricted visibility. Within offshore production, marine transfer and coastal energy infrastructure operating in demanding environmental conditions, this asset can directly influence fatigue, corrosion, difficult access, marine exposure and safe offshore operations. A focused integrity programme should therefore connect the design basis, materials, service history, previous findings and current operating condition, so that inspection effort is directed to credible damage locations and produces information that supports practical maintenance decisions.

Integrity Focus

Principal considerations and IAIS UAE support

Principal integrity considerations

  • Corrosion, wear, cracking or deterioration associated with the operating environment.
  • Damage at welds, connections, supports and mechanically or thermally stressed locations.
  • Coating breakdown, deformation and defects arising from ageing, modification or maintenance history.

How IAIS UAE may support

  • Review available design, service and inspection information to define critical areas and acceptance requirements.
  • Apply a suitable combination of visual, dimensional, NDT, materials and specialised-access methods within the approved scope.
  • Assess and report findings consistently to support repair, monitoring, engineering review and maintenance prioritisation.
Outputs & Requirements

Typical outputs and applicable standards

Typical outputs

Inspection report, asset register, calibrated measurements, NDT records, photographs, anomaly ranking and recommended actions.

Codes & standards

The activity may be planned and reported against the applicable provisions of API offshore and pipeline practices, ISO 19900-series standards, DNV standards, ASME B31 series, ASTM and operator requirements. Final references and acceptance criteria remain subject to approved project and statutory requirements.
