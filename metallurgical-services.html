Service Overview

Practical inspection support for critical industrial assets

IAIS UAE metallurgical services combine material examination, metallography, positive material identification and failure-analysis methods to investigate material condition and damage. The output supports root-cause review, repair decisions, material verification and quality investigations.

Our team scopes the inspection around the asset, access conditions, project requirements and required reporting. The goal is clear, traceable field information that supports maintenance, quality and integrity decisions.
