Service Overview

Practical inspection support for critical industrial assets

IAIS UAE supports asset-integrity programmes that combine inspection history, equipment condition and risk-based planning. The objective is to focus inspection effort on the equipment and degradation mechanisms that matter most while maintaining traceable engineering records.

Our team scopes the inspection around the asset, access conditions, project requirements and required reporting. The goal is clear, traceable field information that supports maintenance, quality and integrity decisions.
