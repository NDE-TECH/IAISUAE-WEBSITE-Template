Sector Overview

Inspection planned around the risks and assets of Power Plants

IAIS UAE supports power-generation maintenance and outage programmes with NDT, tube inspection, heat treatment, mechanical testing and integrity assessment. Inspection scopes are planned around outage windows and equipment criticality.

Scopes can combine field inspection, difficult-access methods, materials testing and integrity support depending on the equipment condition, maintenance window and reporting requirement.
