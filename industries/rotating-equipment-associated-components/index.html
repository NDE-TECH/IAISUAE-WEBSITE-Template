Application & Asset Overview

Rotating-equipment associated components

NDT, materials and condition-assessment support for accessible shafts, casings, welds, supports and connected components. Within continuous and batch process facilities handling elevated temperatures, pressures and aggressive media, this asset can directly influence loss of containment, ageing mechanisms, shutdown readiness and reliable process performance. A focused integrity programme should therefore connect the design basis, materials, service history, previous findings and current operating condition, so that inspection effort is directed to credible damage locations and produces information that supports practical maintenance decisions.

Integrity Focus

Principal considerations and IAIS UAE support

Principal integrity considerations

  • Fatigue cracking, wear, erosion, fretting and surface damage in stressed components.
  • Misalignment, looseness, vibration-related deterioration and defects at shafts, casings, blades or attachments.
  • Material degradation or repair-related discontinuities that may affect safe and reliable operation.

How IAIS UAE may support

  • Select target areas using maintenance history, operating data, OEM guidance and known failure modes.
  • Coordinate cleaning, disassembly status and access, then apply visual, surface, ultrasonic or other suitable NDT.
  • Correlate inspection findings with condition-monitoring or maintenance observations when data are available.
Outputs & Requirements

Typical outputs and applicable standards

Typical outputs

Component-level NDT report, defect locations and sizing, photographic record, acceptance status and maintenance recommendations.

Codes & standards

The activity may be planned and reported against the applicable provisions of API 510, API 570, API 571, API 579-1/ASME FFS-1, API 580/581, ASME BPVC, ASME B31.3, ASTM, ISO and client requirements. Final references and acceptance criteria remain subject to approved project and statutory requirements.
