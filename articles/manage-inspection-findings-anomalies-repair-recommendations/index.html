Managing Inspection Findings, Anomalies and Repair Recommendations

Anomaly management is the controlled journey from detection to final resolution. It ensures that an inspection indication is validated, assessed, prioritised and closed with evidence while temporary risk controls remain visible.

Anomaly management is the controlled journey from detection to final resolution. It ensures that an inspection indication is validated, assessed, prioritised and closed with evidence while temporary risk controls remain visible.

Separate indication from condition

An NDT indication is a response requiring evaluation; it is not automatically a defect. Confirm location, dimensions, method limitations and relevant acceptance basis. Additional examination may be necessary before severity is assigned. For UAE and GCC facilities, the final approach should also reflect local regulatory obligations, the owner’s engineering practices, environmental exposure and the practical access available during operation or shutdown.

In practical application, separate indication from condition should be translated into documented requirements for the relevant assets and responsible teams. For this subject, the priority is to ensure that inspection anomaly management leads to an auditable decision, appropriate field or engineering activity, and a defined response when results fall outside the approved basis. Records should remain linked to the asset so future reviews can compare assumptions with actual condition and operating experience.

Take immediate safeguards

Where a finding may threaten safe operation, define interim controls such as isolation, reduced pressure, increased monitoring or restricted access. The responsible operating authority should understand the assumptions and expiry of those controls. The technical basis should identify the affected equipment, assumptions, uncertainties, responsible authority and evidence required to close the resulting action.

Assess technical significance

Compare the condition with applicable code requirements and determine whether FFS or specialist analysis is needed. Consider damage mechanism, possible extent, growth rate and similar equipment. Record the decision basis and reviewer. This keeps the work focused on a decision and prevents a completed inspection or calculation from being mistaken for a fully controlled integrity outcome.

Write executable recommendations

State the affected component, required action, technical acceptance, priority and completion date. Avoid vague instructions such as monitor closely. Identify prerequisites, inspection hold points and post-repair verification. Where information is incomplete, the uncertainty should be recorded and addressed through verification, conservative assumptions, additional monitoring or appropriately timed examination.

Control deferrals and temporary repairs

Deferral should require documented justification, current condition, risk, safeguards, revised due date and approval. Temporary repairs need design and inspection controls plus a permanent-resolution plan; they should never disappear into routine operation. Applicable codes and standards provide important structure, but their relevance, edition and acceptance requirements must be confirmed for the asset, jurisdiction and client-approved scope.

Close and learn

Verify completion records, NDT, testing, updated drawings and operating restoration. Update RBI, corrosion plans and similar-asset reviews where appropriate. Closure means the risk is resolved or formally transferred to a new controlled action. The strongest result is a traceable connection between the observed condition, its engineering significance and the action that protects continued operation.
