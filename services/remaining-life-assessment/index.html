Service Overview

Remaining-Life Assessment

Remaining-Life Assessment is delivered to determine whether equipment containing degradation can continue operating safely under defined conditions. IAIS UAE develops the scope around the equipment design, materials, service environment, operating history, expected damage mechanisms and the client’s required decision. This prevents technique-led inspection from becoming disconnected from asset risk and ensures that findings are traceable, technically relevant and suitable for maintenance or engineering review.

Category context: This category brings inspection, engineering, operating and maintenance information into one coordinated integrity-management process. Its purpose is not simply to generate more inspection data, but to determine which assets and degradation mechanisms matter most, what information is required, when intervention is justified and how continued operation can be supported responsibly. The work may be applied to a single critical item, an operating unit or a wider portfolio covering static equipment, piping, tanks, structures and reliability-critical machinery.
Integrity Context

Key considerations for this service category

01

Integrity consideration

Inspection programmes that repeat historical coverage without reflecting current risk or active damage mechanisms.

02

Integrity consideration

Incomplete, inconsistent or disconnected design, inspection, corrosion, process and maintenance data.

03

Integrity consideration

Ageing assets, changing operating conditions, temporary repairs and deferred maintenance requiring defensible prioritisation.

04

Integrity consideration

The need to translate technical findings into practical actions, operating limits, repair priorities and inspection intervals.

Category Delivery

How IAIS UAE approaches the work

IAIS UAE may begin with an asset and data review, establish the governing design and operating basis, identify credible deterioration, assess consequence and condition, and then build an integrity plan proportionate to risk. RBI, FFS, corrosion management, remaining-life assessment, integrity operating windows, reliability information and failure investigation can be combined where the decision requires more than one discipline. Recommendations are issued with assumptions, limitations, data gaps and action ownership clearly identified.

01

Typical assets

Pressure vessels, process piping, pipelines, storage tanks, boilers, heat exchangers, structures, rotating-equipment systems and integrated industrial facilities.

02

Client value

Clients receive a prioritised and auditable basis for allocating inspection and maintenance resources, managing anomalies, supporting shutdown decisions and planning repair, replacement or life extension. The category is particularly valuable where many isolated reports exist but a single, risk-informed asset position is required.

03

Typical deliverables

Asset hierarchy and criticality review; damage-mechanism register; risk ranking; inspection and monitoring plan; FFS or remaining-life assessment; corrosion-control actions; integrity operating limits; anomaly register; management dashboard; life-extension roadmap and technical recommendations..

04

Expected outcome

Clear, traceable technical information that supports safety, compliance, reliability, maintenance prioritisation and lifecycle value.
