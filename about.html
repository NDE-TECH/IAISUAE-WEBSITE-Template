Our Story

From an Indian inspection house to a regional integrity partner

Founded in 2005 as Industrial Analysis & Inspection Services, IAIS built its reputation across India's refineries, petrochemical plants and marine yards on one principle: never compromise the data. As GCC operators sought the same rigour closer to home, IAIS UAE was established in Abu Dhabi to deliver that standard directly across the UAE and wider Gulf — sharing personnel training, quality systems and accreditation scope with our India headquarters at iaisindia.com.

Today, the IAIS Group operates across 25+ countries with more than 150 certified engineers, technicians and metallurgists — but the working principle hasn't changed since day one.
