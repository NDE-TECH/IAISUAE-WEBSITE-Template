Technical Article

Building an Integrated Industrial Inspection and Integrity Platform reflects IAIS UAE’s objective of supporting industrial owners with coordinated inspection, integrity and technical services. The organisation is being positioned around evidence-based decisions, recognised technical frameworks and responsive regional support.

Why this matters

Industrial operators need inspection information that can be converted into safe, practical maintenance and lifecycle decisions. IAIS UAE aims to connect field evidence, damage knowledge, asset records and engineering review rather than presenting individual methods as isolated activities. This foundation story must be checked against the company’s final licence, organisation and approved website claims before release.

Our technical direction

The service architecture covers asset integrity, NDT, static equipment, tube inspection, materials and corrosion, marine and offshore, remote inspection, quality surveillance and technical manpower support. Actual delivery remains subject to licensing, approved scope, competence, equipment, procedures and client requirements. Regional relevance

UAE and GCC facilities operate across oil and gas, power, water, marine, manufacturing, infrastructure and emerging energy sectors. Each sector requires an application-led scope based on equipment, service, credible degradation, access and the decision the client must make. Responsible positioning

Codes and standards are applied only where relevant to the asset and contract. Their mention does not represent certification or accreditation. IAIS UAE will publish verified capabilities and avoid unsubstantiated claims about clients, projects or approvals.
