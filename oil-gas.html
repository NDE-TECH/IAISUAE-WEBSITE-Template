Sector Overview

Inspection planned around the risks and assets of Oil & Gas

IAIS UAE supports oil and gas operators with NDT, corrosion assessment, access solutions and integrity programmes for high-pressure, high-consequence equipment. Work can be planned for routine maintenance, turnarounds, project construction and in-service integrity campaigns.

Scopes can combine field inspection, difficult-access methods, materials testing and integrity support depending on the equipment condition, maintenance window and reporting requirement.
